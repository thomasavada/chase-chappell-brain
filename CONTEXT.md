# Shared language

Use these terms. Do not paraphrase them into longer sentences.

**Andromeda** — Meta’s retrieval/indexing update. Unique *formats* and cohorts, not headline swaps. Retrieval 3–4 days only with CAPI matchback ≥ 8–10.

**33% rule** — In one ad set: in-house / UGC / AI × TOF / MOF / BOF (5+5+5). Spend leveled = working. Do not kill the 2x unaware ad.

**Hierarchical indexing** — Andromeda ranks unique formats (review, demo, comparison, gift-cohort), not variants of one winner.

**Matchback** — Event Match Quality / CAPI + Advanced Matching score. Floor **≥ 8–10** or Andromeda retrieval dies.

**4-campaign** — Creative Testing → Interest Testing → Advantage+ (winners, broad) → Retargeting. Few campaigns, many creatives.

**Break-even ROAS** — The number that decides cut / watch / scale. Typical claimed 1.7–1.8. Above ~3x = margin/AOV problem, not “need more ads.”

**$35 AOV floor** — Below $35, scale is “horrible.” Double AOV ≈ double ROAS without touching ads.

**L1 / L2 / L3** — Three levels of purchase. L1 front-end profit (caps). L2 AOV (still caps). L3 come-back: subscription + loyalty (claimed ~17x LTV ROAS at 7 repeats).

**Ads own CPM+CTR** — If both are fine and ROAS/CVR is not, the job is site / AOV / returning / offer, not Meta.

**Flat sales + high ROAS** — Usually ads hitting existing customers. Exclude past purchasers from acquisition.

**Partnership Hub** — Tagged photo/video history = ad-ready paid collab, often 0 commission.

**Euka** — Meta UGC army at **5%** of ad-attributed sales (not 20% TTS). 500 emails/day. Creators submit assets, not shoppable TTS.

**GPM** — TikTok Shop: sales per 1,000 views. Screen creators on GPM + views, not 30-day GMV alone.

**Request rate** — TTS invites that become sample requests. 0.1% is dead; ~5% is the gate.

**Content refinery** — A 0-view sample video is not trash. One asset → Meta test, GMV Max, site, email.

**Villain marketing** — Attention > approval. Controversy is distribution; Shop + PDP monetize it.

**Operator** — Funnel as one flywheel. Opposite of technical (one button) and manager (siloed teams).

**Creative-to-spend ratio** — New unique ads must keep up with spend. +10% creatives / +131% spend kills ROAS.

**Claimed** — Any Chase scale, ad-count, or GMV figure. Not audited here.
