# Glossary

Canonical terms. See also repo `CONTEXT.md`.

## Economics

**Break-even ROAS** — Revenue / spend at which you neither make nor lose money on ads. Typical claimed 1.7–1.8.

**$35 AOV floor** — Average order value below which Chase says scale is nearly impossible.

**L1 / L2 / L3** — [[Three levels of purchase]]: front-end profit → AOV → subscription/LTV.

**MER** — Marketing efficiency ratio: total revenue ÷ total ad spend. Use when channels overlap.

**Claimed** — A Chase figure (scale, ads, GMV) that this vault has not audited.

## Meta

**Andromeda** — Meta retrieval/indexing. Unique formats; retrieval 3–4 days iff [[CAPI matchback]] ≥ 8–10. [[Andromeda stack]]

**Hierarchical indexing** — Ranking unique formats/cohorts, not headline swaps.

**33% rule** — In-house / UGC / AI × TOF / MOF / BOF in **one ad set**. [[33 percent rule]]

**Matchback** — CAPI + Advanced Matching quality. Floor ≥ 8–10.

**4-campaign** — Testing → interest → Advantage+ → retargeting. [[4-campaign method]]

**Partnership Hub** — Tagged history as ad-ready collab. [[Partnership Hub and Euka]]

**Euka** — Meta UGC army at 5% of ad-attributed sales.

## TikTok Shop

**GPM** — Sales per 1,000 views. Creator screen.

**Request rate** — Invites that become sample requests. ~5% is the gate. [[TikTok Shop army]]

**Content refinery** — One video becomes many channel assets.

## Brand

**Villain marketing** — Attention over approval. [[Villain marketing]]

**Positional engineering** — Win the belief before the transaction. [[Five levels of brands]]

**Operator** — Funnel as one flywheel, not siloed teams.

**Diagnostic layer** — Hims-style quiz that pulls a solution, not a product page. [[Hims diagnostic layer]]
