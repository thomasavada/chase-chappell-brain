# The Rise of Bloom Nutrition

- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/yv4VVX49gTA
- **Date:** 2026-11-13 · 19:59 · 1,245 views at fetch
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** Bloom repeatedly exchanged a locally successful asset for the next distribution unlock: founder audience → paid traffic to digital guides → physical products → broader daily-wellness positioning → creator-led demand → public Amazon proof → national retail → beverage distribution. The pattern is not channel expansion for its own sake; each move removes the current category or distribution ceiling while preserving demand creation.

**Claimed progression:** Chase says Mari and Greg sold more than one million $5–$10 guides and more than one million resistance bands; Bloom then became a claimed $50M business through greens and creator seeding and reached $180M by late 2023 after Amazon and retail expansion. He describes Bloom as worth more than $1B, says its first outside raise was $90M for 20%, and claims Bloom Energy sells one million cans per day and uses 10+ third-party plants. These figures are **claimed**, not audited.

**Operating lessons:** Start with an audience problem before committing to infrastructure; broaden the use case when the product category caps demand; use a marketplace ranking as public demand proof when the next buyer is retail; fund retail visibility and time the launch to peak intent; and take strategic capital for capabilities—here, beverage manufacturing and Keurig Dr Pepper distribution—not merely cash. Chase presents TikTok Shop as demand generation even near break-even because he claims a 30–40% spillover into retail and Amazon.

**Creator loop:** Recruit at scale, pay meaningful commission, convene creators in one community, teach from actual selling videos, and feed proven concepts back into new variations. Chase's latest stated snapshot is 56,000+ creators and 230,000+ shoppable videos—roughly four videos per creator—without reconciling it to other Bloom snapshots in the corpus.
