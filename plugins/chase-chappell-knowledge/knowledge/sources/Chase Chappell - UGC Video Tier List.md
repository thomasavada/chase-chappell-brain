# I Ranked Every UGC Video for Meta & TikTok

- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/vtjMMvEcxEw
- **Date:** 2026-09-04 · 27:24 · 6,476 views at fetch
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** Rank creator videos by sales efficiency, not views. Strong videos hook with a visible problem or aesthetic, demonstrate the product and purchase path, state fit/size/use cases, handle objections, show the offer, and repeat a clear CTA with truthful urgency. ASMR or hype can win views while weak demos and absent CTAs under-convert.

**Claimed examples:** Crocs $290K, apparel $500K, Ninja $1.3M and Comfrt $1M from single videos; all sales/view figures and tier judgments are **claimed**, not independently audited.
