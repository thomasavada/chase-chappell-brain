# I Studied Every Ecommerce Offer So You Don’t Have To

- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/RHZ39WfEuM0
- **Date:** 2026-08-21 · 25:02
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** An offer is an economic trade, not a random discount. Give up unit margin only in exchange for a defined downstream gain: higher conversion, a larger cart, or repeat purchases. Bundles and thresholds make the customer worth more now; subscription offers can justify a higher CAC later, but front-loaded losses can exhaust a small brand’s cash.

**Claimed examples:** mystery-offer popups reached roughly 15–18% opt-in; one subscription comparison put six-month customer value at $289 versus $165 without subscription. Figures and case economics are **claimed**, not audited.
