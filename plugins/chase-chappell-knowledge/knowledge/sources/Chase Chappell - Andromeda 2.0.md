# The New Era of Meta Has Just Begun

- **Watch:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pz2OpRD6X8c
- **Date:** 2026-11-21 · 12:09 · 3,869 views at fetch
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** Chase's “Andromeda 2.0” update moves creative strategy from format diversity alone to **motivation diversity**. Give Meta genuinely distinct messages for distinct pains, objections and desires; consolidate audience micromanagement where the account has enough signal; then evaluate creative delivery separately from creative performance.

**System claims:** Chase describes Meta's mixture-of-experts architecture as matching an individual ad to a more specific buyer context. He says full-candidate scoring raises retrieval throughput by approximately **100×** versus the original Andromeda system and attributes a **2.4% conversion lift** to Meta's update. These figures and the “Andromeda 2.0” framing are **claimed**, not independently audited here.

**Five adjustments:** (1) Mine reviews, support, sales calls and customer feedback for buying motivations rather than producing superficial hook variants. (2) Assign each creator or AI-assisted concept one distinct customer problem, objection or desire. (3) Simplify toward broad prospecting plus dedicated testing and scaling instead of encoding every journey stage in audiences. (4) Do not call an ad a loser merely because Meta gave it little delivery; judge both delivery and observed results. (5) Separate profitable scaling from opportunity validation: move proven ads into scaling, while giving promising under-delivered concepts a bounded second test in a revival campaign.

**Testing constraint:** Chase illustrates moving the top **25%** of delivered, proven creatives into scaling and under-delivered creatives into revival, but warns against treating either percentage or volume as universal. Creator-level ad sets can force a fair creator test, provided budget and conversion volume make the result meaningful. A $100/day account should not copy the infrastructure of a $100,000/day account; both spend levels are **claimed examples**, not thresholds.
