# Brand breakdown (how Chase does it)

Chase does not review a brand. He names the **system**. Shorts are 40–90s. Long-form (Hims, David) is the same skeleton with more proof.

Watch a model short: [Hims](https://youtu.be/b6CBT5y5IJ0) · long-form [Hims $8B](https://youtu.be/FVDTvDZ-0TQ) · villain [David](https://youtu.be/Xs_EErafeyY) · volume verdict [Bloom vs Grüns](https://youtu.be/y4ZmR-OKKtU)

## Three levers (almost every short)

1. **Unique Meta ad volume** — count + format diversity, not “nice creative.” Head-to-head winner = more unique ads ([[33 percent rule]]).
2. **Creator army** — TTS affiliates or Meta Hub/Euka, not one celebrity. Alix Earle + 27 ads still loses ([[Brand atlas]] Reale vs Prequel).
3. **TikTok Shop as distribution** — shoppable volume, not a sales channel.

## The hook line

“They’re not a [category] brand. They’re a [machine].”

Examples he uses (claimed):

- Hims — not pills. Diagnostic system. Sell self-esteem/dating.
- Medicube — not skincare. Creator-powered money machine.
- David Protein — not protein. Attention company.
- Mars Men — sell belly fat / T / man boobs, not the bottle.

## Output skeleton (fill every row)

| Slot | Ask |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | What do they sell instead of the SKU? |
| **Scale** | Revenue / ads / TTS GMV — tag **claimed** |
| **Ads** | Unique active Meta ads. Formats: UGC, demo, review, B&A, founder, offer? Spend leveled or 1–2 winners? |
| **Army** | TTS affiliates + shoppable videos, or Meta collab/Euka, or face-only celebrity? |
| **Funnel** | Quiz? Sub default? First-box stacked offer? AOV ladder? |
| **Level** | 1 Survival … 5 Icon ([[Five levels of brands]]). Founder story vs forgotten founder. |
| **Rival** | Nearest competitor. Who has more unique ads? That is the verdict. |
| **One job** | If you steal the *system* (not the product), what do you do Monday? |
| **Watch** | Chase short if in [[Brand atlas]]; else Meta Ads Library |

## Traps he calls

- Celebrity without ad volume (Reale, Kylie face-only, As Ever).
- 12 Meta ads while the rival runs 790 (Bloom vs Grüns).
- Clean PDP beating a worse army — Prima can win conversion and still lose distribution (David).
- “Whiter teeth” / “you have hair loss” — category language. He wants the desire.
