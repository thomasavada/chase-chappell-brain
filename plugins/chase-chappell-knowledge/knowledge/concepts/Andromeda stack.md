## “Andromeda 2.0” overlay

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pz2OpRD6X8c). Chase's November 2026 update adds three operating rules:

1. **Motivation diversity, not variant volume.** Keep format and funnel diversity, but make each concept answer one distinct pain, objection or desire found in reviews, support tickets, sales calls and customer feedback. Five creators reading one script is not five ideas.
2. **Consolidate targeting; specialize messages.** Chase says Meta's mixture-of-experts architecture can match more specific messages to buyer context, while full-candidate scoring runs at a claimed **~100×** the original Andromeda retrieval throughput. Prefer broad prospecting plus dedicated creative testing and scaling where account signal supports it; do not infer that 500 ads in one ad set is better.
3. **Separate delivery from performance.** Low or zero delivery is not evidence that an ad failed, and high spend is not evidence that it is profitable. Move meaningfully proven creatives into scaling; give promising under-delivered concepts a bounded revival test. Use creator-specific ad sets only when the experiment needs each creator to receive delivery and the budget can support a meaningful read.

Creative volume must fit spend and conversion volume. Chase's top-25%-to-scaling example and the **100×** retrieval figure are **claimed**, not universal thresholds or independently audited facts.
