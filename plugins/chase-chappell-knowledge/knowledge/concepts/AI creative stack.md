# AI creative stack

Watch: [Claude changed Facebook ads](https://youtu.be/ReYEYoLsppk) · [Claude analysis metrics](https://youtu.be/AKvev3zRH_E) · [Cloned 100 ads](https://youtu.be/80qVcoGUkN4) · [Dangerously good with AI](https://youtu.be/yEneQrnogGA)

AI compresses the stack. It **supplements** real UGC; do not be 100% AI (trust).

Chain: Claude + Shopify connector (site) → brand kit (Higgsfield / Nano Banana) → Imaginative statics (lock kit) → Arcads/Higgsfield UGC → Manus/Yuka/Euka outreach → Textcart SMS.

**Meta Ads MCP (Aug 2026):** Claude Desktop reads the ad account, picks winners by **spend + volume** (5,000 purchases at 4x > 5 purchases at 50x), Higgsfield variants, draft/publish into **creative testing**, weekly scheduler. Auto-publish stays out of big CBO.

**Meta Ads MCP (Sep 2026):** use spend gates before creative generation: flag zero-purchase ads past ~2× average CPP and one-purchase ads past ~2–2.5× CPP; classify every active asset by format and funnel role; identify gaps; retrieve long-running references with Foreplay; generate candidate statics/video with Higgsfield; then create a campaign **draft for human review**. The thresholds and example account results are **claimed**, not universal rules. [Watch](https://youtu.be/AYp982ZpxNQ)

**Meta Ads MCP analysis (Nov 2026):** move beyond Ads Manager's default columns. Give Claude AOV, COGS, shipping and target margin to derive break-even economics; group performance by hook, angle, funnel stage and format; compare 3/7/14/30-day windows to estimate creative win rate, winner spend concentration and fatigue; then surface wasted spend and production gaps. Treat the resulting recommendation as analysis for operator review, not permission to auto-publish. [Watch](https://youtu.be/AKvev3zRH_E)

Creative-to-spend ratio: July Happy Dad claimed +10% new ads / +131% spend → ROAS died.

Hyper-specific tools beat general chat. Brand-kit lock is the moat.
