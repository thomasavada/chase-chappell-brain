# Metric diagnosis tree

Watch: [If you watch one Facebook Ad video](https://youtu.be/42uhZYnyEXU) — classroom: [[Chase classroom]]. Model beat **[t=9:36](https://youtu.be/42uhZYnyEXU?t=936)**.

**Ads own CPM + CTR.** If those are fine and ROAS/CVR is not, do not “run more ads.” Each row is one lesson: trap guess → real job.

| Symptom | Meaning | Job |
|---|---|---|
| High CPM | Compliance keywords and/or weak offer (biggest levers) | Fix language Meta flags; beat competitor offer — named [[Hero mechanism]], not a bigger discount |
| Low CTR | Creative/message not calling the right person | Unique formats, audience call-out — volume of the *same* format does not save you |
| Good CTR+CPM, low CVR | **Site**, not ads | [[Shopify CRO]]: first-load matches the ad; 3 offer bullets; 5–7 images; default sub; offer + free ship above the fold |
| Low CVR + high AOV | Normal (e.g. $1,700 machine) | Don’t panic |
| **Flat sales + high ROAS** (t=9:36) | Trap: spend more. Meaning: ~80% returning; ads + email attribution on the same people. Stuck $40K or $400K/mo | Exclude past buyers + email from acquisition. First-time objective. 15% / 30% first sub **on site and ads**. Returning → ~30% |
| Low returning | No reason to come back | Consumable: **subscription**. Apparel: 2nd–5th hero, drops |
| Low ROAS, rest fine | AOV | Floor **$35** |
| ATC 8% / CVR 1% | Shipping or checkout broken | Price, **drawer** vs page, wallets. Shopify leak rows: [[Shopify CRO]] |
| Low ATC (2–5%) | Button / offer / education | ATC above the fold; contrast; first-load. Not more ads |
| ATC high, reach-checkout dies | Extra pages / popup at cart | Drawer, fewer clicks |
| ATC ≈ CVR | Opportunity to pull ATC with persona PDPs | Don’t stop because “CVR is fine” |
| TTS low request rate | Outreach copy, not listing only | Test 5 invite agents |
| High request + low sales | No GPM screen | Pre/post-qualify |
| ROAS dies on scale | Creative volume didn’t keep up | 3x spend needs ~3x unique ads; new ads in **testing** campaign |

## Blended measurement bound

Platform ROAS is a platform’s attribution claim, not the whole customer journey. A buyer can discover on Meta, validate through a TikTok creator, search on Google, and close on Amazon or retail. Do not shut off a demand-creating channel solely because another channel captured the sale. Chase’s 2026 scorecard prioritizes **blended CAC, new-customer CAC, MER, contribution margin, incremental revenue, total profitable revenue growth, and CAC:LTV**. The example of $60 CAC against $240 LTV is **claimed**, not a forecast. [Watch](https://youtu.be/4g5XZtBnGA8)

## False-attribution audit

When ROAS spikes but Shopify revenue or new-customer volume does not, inspect **Breakdown → Audience segments** before scaling. Define engaged audiences (site/email/social), define existing customers with purchaser history plus the customer list, and separate new audience. For cold acquisition, Chase's current sequence is: exclude purchaser/customer/email lists; test the **acquire new customers** lifecycle objective; compare click-only reporting against view/engagement credit; then test Meta's **incremental attribution** model. Reported ROAS should fall if duplicate credit is removed—that is measurement correction, not necessarily performance decay. Settings and availability can drift; verify them in the live account. Figures in the worked example are **claimed**. [Watch](https://youtu.be/uhH0OZwXrUs)
