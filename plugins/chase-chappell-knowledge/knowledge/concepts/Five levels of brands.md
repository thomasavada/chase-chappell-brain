# Five levels of brands

Watch: [5 levels of brands](https://youtu.be/6IAgiYdUlXM) · [Personal brand](https://youtu.be/VpD-eZXLVfs) · [Grow 20x](https://youtu.be/yEuMxil7-ws)

**Brand:** L1 Survival (founder is salesperson) → L2 Systems (dangerous $100K–500K/mo; pull founder out) → L3 Identity (polarize; who you are *not* for) → L4 Distribution (think like a media company) → L5 Icon. *Level-1 behavior in a level-5 business destroys growth.*

**Positional engineering:** find the industry lie → new binary standard → every touchpoint reinforces it. Win belief before the transaction.

**Personal brand (separate ladder):** audience → founder story (un-copyable; Plants Basically $20K→$600K claimed) → face as accelerant → forgotten founder = winning (Gymshark) → re-entry when winning (Greg/Bloom).
