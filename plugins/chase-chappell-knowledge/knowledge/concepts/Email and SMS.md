# Email and SMS

Watch: [13h course](https://youtu.be/8AAIf5PWJik) (backend). Come-back is **not** only subscription — [[Three levels of purchase]] L3 still owns the first lever when **returning is low**.

Trap (classroom): *sales flat, high ROAS* → do **not** “send more email.” Ads are hitting the same list. Exclude past buyers + email from **acquisition**. [[Metric diagnosis tree]] · [[Chase classroom]]

## Job

Email/SMS is the **owned** come-back loop so Meta can hunt *new* customers. Course claimed **email ~$36–40 per $1**; SMS open **~82%**, redeem **~10x** email.

## Capture (claimed, 13h)

- Full-width popup: **+25–60%** list growth vs a corner widget.
- Quiz/consult: email **after** sunk cost, not before question 1. [[Hims diagnostic layer]]

## Cart flow (claimed, 13h)

| Beat | When | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~15 min | Reminder |
| 2 | ~12 hr | Reminder + proof |
| 3 | Day 3 | **10% off** |
| 4 | Day 5 | Close or stop |

AI SMS on this flow claimed **+$60K** in month one on one account — **claimed**, not a forecast.

## Bound

- Do not spend retarget budget to re-hit the same list you already email. [[4-campaign method]]
- Apparel come-back is 2nd–5th hero / drops, not a monthly bar. Consumable come-back is **subscription first**, then this loop.
