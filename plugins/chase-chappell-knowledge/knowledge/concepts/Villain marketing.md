# Villain marketing

Watch: [What EXACTLY is Villain Marketing?](https://youtu.be/Xs_EErafeyY)

Being ignored is more dangerous than being disliked. Case: **David Protein** — attention company disguised as a protein brand (claimed ~$300M).

Loop: controversy → algorithm + creators → shoppable video → PDP monetizes (default sub, carton, gamified cart $39 → $140) → revenue funds more campaigns. Even negative comments feed the funnel.

Vs Prima: David wins army + ad volume + TTS ($3M vs $193K claimed). Prima wins cleaner PDP / higher AOV.
