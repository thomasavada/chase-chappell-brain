# Awareness ladder

Watch: [Facebook ad creatives in 2027](https://youtu.be/jDt1eCJpfO0?t=546) (**t=9:06**)

Fill hierarchical indexing with one ad set, five stages:

| Stage | Job | Formats Chase names |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Educate the category | Edu, stat, founder |
| Problem-aware | Make the pain specific | Before/after, problem |
| Solution-aware | Us vs them, proof | UGC, comparison |
| Product-aware | Offer | Bundle, guarantee |
| Most-aware | Direct buy | Price, CTA, unboxing |

Andromeda shows the right stage. You do not audience-split the ladder.
