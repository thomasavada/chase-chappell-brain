# Chase classroom

How he teaches in [If you watch one Facebook Ad video](https://youtu.be/42uhZYnyEXU?t=936) — **t=9:36** is the model beat. `/teach-chase` copies this, not a lecture.

## The loop

1. One **metric combo** (not a topic name).
2. Ask “what does that mean / what do they do?”
3. The intern’s guess is usually **run more ads / spend more / better creative**.
4. Kill that. Ads only own **CPM + CTR**.
5. One **job**. Then the reverse scenario.

## Model beat (t=9:36)

**Combo:** flat sales + high ROAS.

**Trap:** spend more (metrics look good).

**Meaning:** Shopify returning-customer rate is high (he uses **80%**). Campaigns keep hitting the same people. Email sale gets attributed to Meta. Stuck at $40K or $400K/month. High returning is *good for LTV*, bad if *ads* only hit them.

**Job:** Exclude past customers + email list from acquisition. Objective = first-time. Offer first-time (15% off / **30% first sub**) **on the site and in the ads**. Returning ~80% → ~30%, new ~70%, sales start to climb.

**Reverse (t=10:25):** low returning → **subscription** on a consumable, not “better flows” as the first lever. Clothing: 2nd–5th hero + drops, not Recharge.
