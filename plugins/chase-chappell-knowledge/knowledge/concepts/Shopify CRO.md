# Shopify CRO

Watch: [How to increase conversion rates on Shopify](https://youtu.be/daDsoHXE52A). Classroom combo is still **good CTR + CPM, low CVR → site**, not ads. [[Metric diagnosis tree]]

Claimed Shopify average CVR **1.4%**. 4x that without extra spend (claimed: $50k/mo → $200k). Ads own **CPM + CTR** only.

## Shopify leak (one row → one job)

| Drop | Meaning | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Low **add to cart** | Offer, education, or the button | ATC **above the fold**; contrast (not white-on-white); 3 offer bullets; 5–7 images |
| ATC high, **reach checkout** dies | Extra pages / friction | **Drawer**, not a cart page. No popup at checkout. Fewer clicks |
| Reach checkout high, **complete** dies | Checkout | Price, shipping threshold, wallets, trust badges, ship-time |

**First-load:** the PDP hero must be the same visual as the ad they clicked. Mismatch = bounce.

## Speed (claimed)

**1 second delay → ~7% CVR drop.** Compress images (he names Tiny Image Optimizer). High-res still, just not slow.

## Offer above the fold

Everyday Dose / Graza / Carpe as examples: hero offer visible without scroll, subscribe-and-save **on**, quantity/bundle in one view, drawer with upsells, strike-through + free-ship bar. L2 AOV still lives in [[Three levels of purchase]] — this note is the *site* that makes L2 clickable.

Chase also says “hero mechanism” here for that **stack**. The *named why it works* (Whoop recovery score, Rhode glazing) lives in [[Hero mechanism]] — do not collapse.

## Owned recovery (do not duplicate)

Cart sitting in Shopify + no SMS/email = leak, not an ads problem. Capture + cart beats: [[Email and SMS]]. Claimed Heights District **+$60K** month one SMS (same family as the 13h AI-SMS claim — **claimed**, not a forecast).

## Bound

- Do not “run more ads” when CTR+CPM are fine.
- Do not build a second email playbook here.
- Affiliate apps in the video are pointers, not the doctrine.
