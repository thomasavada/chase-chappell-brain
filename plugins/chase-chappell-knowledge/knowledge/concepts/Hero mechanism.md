# Hero mechanism

Watch: [How to craft a $100M offer](https://youtu.be/mJ57WQM_eCo). Nothing else in the ad matters if the offer doesn't convert. Most brands compete on price, features, spend, or discount. The ones that don't: they **name** why this works when everything else hasn't.

**Hero mechanism** = the unique thing inside the offer that explains success. Features sell the watch. Whoop sells a **recovery score**. Rhode sells **glazing formula**. Olaplex **bond-building**. Gravity Blanket **deep pressure**. Glossier Futuredew **oil-serum hybrid**. None were first in category; they were first to name the mechanism.

> Phrase collision: the [CRO video](https://youtu.be/daDsoHXE52A) uses “hero mechanism” for the *PDP offer stack* (discount + gifts above the fold). That job is [[Shopify CRO]]. This note is the **named why**. Do not collapse them.

## Preconditions

Sell into a market that is **begging** (he uses health/wellness — claimed **$6.8T** global). Begging markets are **intelligent**: they have already seen every competitor ad. Some solutions did nothing, some kind of worked, some worked then stopped. You must know the *underlying* issue, not the surface want (feel better / look younger).

## Three questions (before you name anything)

1. Why does the ICP have this problem in the first place?
2. What have they already tried? List it.
3. Why do other products fail that list?

## Craft

1. How does *this* product uniquely hit the **root cause** (ingredient, formulation, process)?
2. Why is it superior to what they already tried?
3. How do we present it as **new** — “newly discovered,” “first time,” “proprietary,” “patent,” “groundbreaking.”
4. **Name it** so it sticks.
5. **Back it.** Authority (derm, scientist) *or*, if you have none, a **common source**: busy mom, trainer, 9-to-5, cured teenager — someone who stumbled on the discovery.

IM8's 90-day protocol ([[Three levels of purchase]] / Sell for 8X) is a hero mechanism as *transformation*, not powder. Hims quiz ([[Hims diagnostic layer]]) personalizes before the offer; it is not a substitute for naming the why. Positional engineering (industry lie → new standard) is the *belief*; this is the *offer name* that carries it.
