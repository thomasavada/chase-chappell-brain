# Break-even ROAS and AOV floor

Watch: [If you watch one Facebook Ad video](https://youtu.be/42uhZYnyEXU)

**Break-even ROAS** is the first number. Most stores Chase cites sit at **1.7–1.8**. If you need **> ~3x** to break even, ads are not the bottleneck — margin or AOV is.

**$35 AOV floor.** Below that, scale is “horrible and almost impossible.” Doubling AOV $35 → $70 can take ROAS 1.2 → 2.4 **without changing ads**.

AOV ladder (claimed): $20 → bundle $35 → cross-sell popup $55 → in-cart $120 → post-purchase $160. Shipping threshold **$1 above current AOV**.

Goal is not max ROAS. Goal is **lowest ROAS you can afford at the highest new-customer volume you can handle**.

Paint columns: red ≤ break-even (cut), yellow break-even → +1x (watch), green ≥ +1x (scale).
