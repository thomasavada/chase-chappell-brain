# Partnership Hub and Euka

Watch: [Hub](https://youtu.be/EH1DBGsShbU) · [Euka / 10X](https://youtu.be/ih17rzW5JK0?t=60)

**Partnership Ads Hub:** every photo/video that tagged the brand is an ad-ready paid collab, often **0 commission**. Brands sitting 2–6 years have a free library. V2 = Creator Market Hub. Claimed client $40K → $100K in <30 days.

**Euka:** Meta UGC army **without TTS**. Creators submit assets. Commission **5% of ad-attributed sales** (not 20% TTS). Max payout per video. Brand HQ + Discord + dynamic brief. Instagram search ≥1,000 views. **500 emails/day**.

Order: pixel/CAPI → sweep Hub → stand up Euka. Hub ≠ army ≠ indexing.

## Cross-platform ambassador loop

The updated Yuka walkthrough separates two contracts: an **organic affiliate program** (example: 10% of link/code sales) and a licensed **Meta partnership-ad program** (example: another 5% of ad-attributed sales, optionally capped per video). Embed the application in Shopify; recruit from Instagram/YouTube lists and existing TikTok Shop creators; send samples; track organic conversion; request usage rights only after content arrives; then promote proven posts and return winner data to creators. Rates and outcomes are **claimed examples**, not defaults. [Watch](https://youtu.be/yvco21OOxpM)
