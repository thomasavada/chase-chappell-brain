# TikTok Shop army

Watch: [Bloom $17M](https://youtu.be/ijeGs0XCwso) · [$0 to $315K](https://youtu.be/fGxGIQWv-6I)

TTS is a **distribution ecosystem**, not a sales channel. One creator video → Meta, Reels, Amazon, email, PDP. Halo ~**30%** claimed onto Amazon/Shopify/retail.

- Listing quality throttles creator video. Fix images, title, reviews, packs first.
- **Request rate** 0.1% is dead; ~**5%** is the gate. Test 5 outreach agents.
- **GPM** = sales / 1,000 views. Screen on GPM + views, not 30-day GMV.
- $100K/month claimed path: **400 samples/week**. 100K invites/month → ~6% request → ~1,200 samples.
- Affiliate-to-video **3:1–5:1**. Discord/WhatsApp army: education + announcements.
- **Content refinery:** 0-view sample is not trash. Organic → Meta test → GMV Max → site/email.
- **10% rule** (claimed, [13h](https://youtu.be/8AAIf5PWJik)): a model organic video at ~10% of views is the one you lift into ads / GMV Max — not the 0-view pile.

## TikTok Ads (paid — not Shop)

Different job from the army. Course claimed: native (not Meta-looking) +~27% completion; interest **stack**; CTR **>2%**, CPM **<$30**; ~7 creatives per audience; refresh 3–5/week. Creative science (claimed): **5+ scenes +171% CVR**, caption +80%, voiceover+offer +87%, low-fi +33%. Credit range claimed $1,500–6,000.

Do not diagnose a dead **Shop** request rate with these paid-ad numbers. Request rate is outreach. These numbers are for **TikTok ads**.
